Methods

We use autonomous acoustic recorders suited to marine and terrestrial sites. Details below are placeholders for your protocol text.

SoundTrap

Underwater acoustic recorder for marine / estuary deployments (e.g. Tai O). High sample rates for fish, invertebrates, and vessel noise.

AudioMoth

Low-cost open recorder for terrestrial and shoreline sites. Useful for birds, insects, and ambient soundscapes (e.g. Lung Fu Shan).

Song Meter SM4

Wildlife Acoustics SM4 for longer terrestrial deployments with stereo or dual-channel options (e.g. Mui Wo hinterland).
